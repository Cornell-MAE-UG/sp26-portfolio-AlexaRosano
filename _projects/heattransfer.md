# Heat Transfer Portfolio Piece
<h1>Problem Set 3 Reflection</h1>

<p>
So far in MAE 3240, the most valuable homework assignment to me was Problem Set 3. It was one of the first assignments where I was able to apply multiple concepts together, such as the concept of energy conservation and using circuits as a way to represent thermal resistance. Additionally, it was good practice in applying multiple forms of heat transfer in succession, which gave me the opportunity to understand how they differed from one another and how they interact within the same system. In particular, deriving the governing heat equation and then applying it to layered materials helped connect the theoretical equations to practical heat transfer problems. This problem set helped create a foundation that I’ve used in all the following assignments.
</p>

<p>
This assignment will be most useful to me not because of its complexity, but because it helped me build a strong conceptual foundation as I further practice heat transfer and begin applying these ideas to more complicated problems. It also reinforced how important it is to clearly understand the basic principles before moving on to more advanced topics. Working on harder concepts is impossible without a fundamental understanding of where everything stems from, and this assignment helped strengthen that understanding early in the course. Understanding how to model heat flow using energy balances and thermal resistance networks is a skill that will be directly useful when analyzing real engineering systems such as insulated structures, electronics cooling, and heat exchangers.
</p>
